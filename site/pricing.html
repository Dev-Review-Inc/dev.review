Pricing

Charge for the client, never for the pipe.

What is sold is seats: multi-device sync and shared sources, over storage you already own. The bytes ride storage that is yours, so there is no pipe of ours to meter.

Seats

Per seat, for multi-device sync and shared sources. A team sharing a draft source is multi-device sync with more than one person on it, so it is one thing sold one way.

The bytes ride storage you already own.

Which is why the rule holds. Drafts and decisions sync through your own folder or your own bucket, so there is no pipe of ours to meter and no tier at which we hold your files. The subscription buys the client that syncs and coordinates, not the carrying. The only thing on a server of ours is a seat record, which is a customer list rather than customer code.
